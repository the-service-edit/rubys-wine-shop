02 · Two quick calls for you

Tell me these and I'll set it up.

The tick box on your forms

Unticked (my recommendation): people tick it to join, so every subscriber chose you. Or ticked: they untick it if they don't want it. More sign-ups, but the ACMA (Australia's regulator) advises against it, so it's your call.

Who counts as a regular

My recommendation: three or more visits in the past year, the last within six months. One-off buyers aren't emailed; they're asked at the counter instead.

Why this lineThe Spam Act lets you email people you have a genuine, ongoing relationship with, about what they already buy from you. The ACMA says a single purchase isn't enough. Anyone who unsubscribes is removed straight away. I'm not a lawyer; this follows the ACMA's published guidance.
