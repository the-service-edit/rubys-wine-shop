Sending it from Shopify

  1. Tap Copy for Shopify at the top.
  2. On your computer, open Shopify Email, create a new email and choose Code your own.
  3. Select everything in the code box, paste over it, and save.
  4. Copy the subject line and preview text from the left into Shopify's fields.
  5. Send a test to yourself, check it on your phone, then schedule it.

Want to change something after pasting? Change it here and paste again. Your latest email is saved in this browser automatically.
